VERDICT: through layers 0-9 the 1B representation is near-perfectly
linearly translatable into the 3B space on BOTH hallucination
and control prompts — the small model's early representations are not the
problem. Translatability then falls off from layer 10 (onset), and on
hallucination prompts it drops further than on controls, with the largest
hallucination-specific gap at layer 12 (gap -0.048).
Alignment thus localises the divergence to the layers around 12,
the candidate root-cause region for the hallucination.
