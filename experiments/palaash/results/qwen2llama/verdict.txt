VERDICT: through layers 0-0 the Qwen-0.5B representation is near-perfectly
linearly translatable into the Llama-3B space on BOTH hallucination
and control prompts — the small model's early representations are not the
problem. Translatability then falls off from layer 1 (onset), and on
hallucination prompts it drops further than on controls, with the largest
hallucination-specific gap at layer 9 (gap -0.227).
Alignment thus localises the divergence to the layers around 9,
the candidate root-cause region for the hallucination.
Its depth-matched counterpart is Llama-3B layer 8 — that is the
layer an adapter would stitch into.
