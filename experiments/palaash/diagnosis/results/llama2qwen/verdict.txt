VERDICT: through layers 0-0 the Llama-1B representation is near-perfectly
linearly translatable into the Qwen-3B space on BOTH hallucination
and control prompts — the small model's early representations are not the
problem. Translatability then falls off from layer 1 (onset), and on
hallucination prompts it drops further than on controls, with the largest
hallucination-specific gap at layer 1 (gap -0.590).
Alignment thus localises the divergence to the layers around 1,
the candidate root-cause region for the hallucination.
Its depth-matched counterpart is Qwen-3B layer 2 — that is the
layer an adapter would stitch into.
